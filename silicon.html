SILICON

You should pick who gets to control your browser.

Silicon exists to be locked down first, and you choose to allow proxies or anything else that compromises your control.

The idea

Silicon (named after by silica aerogel, a see through and lightweight element) is supposed to be a browser that gives the user ultimate control, where nothing happens without you being asked first. Ideally, it will be lightweight, not take up too much RAM, and stop any type of malware or spyware that tries to attack your computer.

Safety is important

Atomic's main concern is that the modern internet isn't safe at all. No matter by who, your information is constantly being spread throughout the internet

Help test

Testing on different machines is valuable. If you find a bug or have hardware information to share, use the repository's issue tracker and include the device, relevant logs, and steps to reproduce the problem.
