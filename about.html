ABOUT CESIUM

More control.
Fewer compromises.

Cesium is an independent operating system project exploring what a flexible, capable desktop system can be. So how much do you think this advanced operating enviornment is worth?

Wait just one moment before you answer...

There are plenty of operating systems. The goal of Cesium isn't to make another version of what already exists just for the sake of it. It's to explore how the pieces of a computer system can fit together in a way that gives users more freedom and fewer unnecessary limitations.

A broader project

Cesium OS is the starting point, but Cesium is intended to become a wider suite of software. Applications, utilities, and other projects may be added over time as they become real and useful. The site navigation is designed to make room for that growth without pretending those projects already exist.

Built in the open

Development happens publicly. The source repository is the best place to follow changes, inspect the work, and see what is currently implemented.
