Cesium is an operating system project built around compatibility, modularity, and giving people more control over their computers.

01 / THE PROJECT

A system built to make sense.

Operating systems shouldn't make you fight your own hardware or lock you into one way of working. Cesium explores a more flexible approach: a system that can adapt to different needs without losing sight of the fundamentals.
